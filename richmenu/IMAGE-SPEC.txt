Goes with richmenu.json in this folder. Drafted 2026-09-28; six buttons since
the LINE door build the same day.

FILE
  2500 x 1686 pixels, PNG or JPEG, under 1 MB.
  Two rows of three panels. Columns 833, 834 and 833 wide; rows 843 high.
  The tap areas in richmenu.json match these panels exactly.

LOOK
  Bright: background #fffdf6, ink #1d1b16, accent #d62828 (the page colours).
  A 4 px #eee6d4 rule between panels.
  Thai on top, large (about 120 px, bold). English under it (about 80 px).
  One simple line drawing per panel above the words, in the accent colour,
  about 220 px tall. Drawings, not emoji.

PANELS
  top row, left to right
  1  ฝนตกไหม      Raining?    a cloud with three slanted rain strokes
  2  น้ำท่วม        Flood       three wavy lines, a short post marked at knee height
  3  ใกล้ฉัน        Near me     a map pin
  bottom row, left to right
  4  ปลอดภัย       Safe        a house outline with a tick inside

WHAT EACH BUTTON DOES (src/pages.js), and the var it needs
  1  a=rain      PAGES or SPOTTERS  asks none / light / heavy, then forwards to
                                    mot-dang-claims /rain with the last shared
                                    location, or asks for a location first
  2  a=flood     PAGES or SPOTTERS  dry / ankle / knee / waist / chest / roof, same path
  3  a=near      ASK                nearest pharmacy, fuel, toilet, hospital,
                                    7-Eleven; with ASK off and PAGES on, a link
                                    to motdang.net/here.html
  4  a=safe      SAFE               two buttons: safe, or need help
  5  a=passport  STAMPS             the Mae Hong Son loop or the nine wats
  6  a=page      PAGES              the sender's page link
  A button whose var is off gets no answer.
